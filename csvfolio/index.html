What can I open in CSV Folio?

Import CSV data from a file, another app’s share menu, pasted text or an HTTP/HTTPS URL. Comma, tab, semicolon and space delimiters are supported, along with automatic encoding detection and a manual encoding selector.

Will editing change my original file?

Edits, sorting and filters apply to the document imported into CSV Folio. To save your changes to an external file, use Export and choose a destination.

Can I use it offline?

You can view, search and edit data already imported into the app without an internet connection. Importing from a web URL and loading remote images require a connection.

What happens to my data?

CSV data is stored in the app’s private storage on your device, with no feature that uploads it to the developer’s server. The release app uses Firebase Crashlytics for diagnostics. Usage analytics only run with your consent. See the privacy policy for details.

Which Android versions are supported?

CSV Folio supports Android 8.0 and later. Visit the Google Play listing to check availability for your device.
