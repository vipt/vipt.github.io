Privacy policy

May Calendar for Android
Last updated: October 16, 2026

Motoki Takahashi (the developer) explains below how May Calendar for Android (app ID: net.vipt.mcalendar, the app) handles information. This policy applies to the Android edition.

1. Calendar access

The app uses calendar read and write permissions to display, create, edit, delete and search events and show widgets. It handles calendar and account names, event titles, dates, locations, notes, recurrence rules and reminders. Events are stored in Android's Calendar Provider.

Calendars belonging to Google or other accounts sync through the account provider's sync service and settings. The developer does not operate a separate calendar sync server. Device-only calendars do not sync and may be lost when the device or calendar storage is reset. Reminder delivery depends on the calendar app installed on your device and its settings.

2. Information stored by the app

Display preferences, profiles, widget settings, templates, input history and information for input suggestions are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Templates and history include event titles, locations, notes and dates. History is limited to 500 entries; older entries are removed when that limit is exceeded.

The app does not provide its own cloud sync or export for this information. Its settings files are excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Events in Calendar Provider and synced accounts are managed separately.

3. Usage analytics and crash reporting

The release version automatically collects information through Google's Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) and Firebase Crashlytics to understand usage, improve quality and investigate problems. The app currently has no setting to turn this collection on or off.

Analytics: sends usage such as launches, engagement and screen views, app-instance identifiers, and device, operating system and app information to Google. The SDK may collect the advertising ID when available under device settings and infer approximate location from an IP address. The app does not request precise device location permission.

Crashlytics: sends crash times, exceptions, stack traces, device and app state, and installation and session identifiers to Google. Diagnostic reports may include usage preceding a crash.

The app does not add event titles, locations or notes to analytics events or custom logs. However, information contained in automatically captured exception messages can be included in crash reports. The app does not set a custom user ID. The Android edition has no displayed ads or in-app purchases.

4. Recipients and security

Google processes the usage and diagnostic information described above, and the developer uses the resulting reports. Transfers to Firebase are encrypted. Google and its service providers may process information in facilities outside your country. Android app storage and permission controls protect access to information on your device.

Calendar sync providers, Google and X (when used to contact us) also have their own policies. See the Google Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy and security information, Analytics data collection information and X Privacy Policy.

5. Retention and deletion

Events remain until you delete them or the calendar provider removes them under its retention policy. Deleting a synced event may affect other devices. Uninstalling this app does not necessarily remove events from Calendar Provider or your synced accounts.

You can delete templates, history and profiles in their respective management screens. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes preferences and saved data in its private storage. Deleting history or templates is separate from deleting calendar events.

Usage data is retained and deleted according to the retention period configured in Google Analytics and that service's deletion processes. Aggregate reports may follow different retention rules. Under Google's policy, Crashlytics normally retains crash data and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting their removal. Uninstalling the app or revoking permissions does not immediately delete data already sent.

6. Your controls and contact

You can revoke calendar permissions in Android's app settings at any time. This disables features that need calendar access, including event editing and widgets. Revoking calendar permissions does not disable Firebase analytics or crash reporting.

For privacy questions or access, correction and deletion requests, contact the developer on X at @may_calendar. Information in your inquiry is used as needed to review and respond to it. Do not include private event details or personal information in public posts. The app has no separate user account registration, and the developer does not hold a mapping between an individual and the identifiers sent, so it may not be possible to identify the relevant data.

7. Policy updates

We update this policy when features or data practices change and publish it on the website and in the app. The app displays the policy bundled with that app version. The latest published version is available in the Android privacy policy.
