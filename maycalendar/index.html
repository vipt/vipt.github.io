QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Before you get started.

Is it free?

The app is free to download and offers in-app purchases. Check the App Store for current pricing and purchase options. View on the App Store

Does it work on iPad?

Yes, it supports iPhone and iPad, with some widgets exclusive to iPad. Check the App Store for current OS requirements.

Can it show reminders and holidays?

It supports reminders, holidays, week numbers and lunar calendar information. You can also set the first day of the week and the time zone.

Where do notifications come from?

Notifications are delivered by Apple's Calendar app. You can set alerts before an event starts or before it ends.
